A fluid wagon with two flamethrowers on its roof. They burn like flamethrower turrets, also while the train is moving.

![Flamethrower wagon in action](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vertexvaar/factorio-flamethrower-turret-wagon/main/previews/portal/1-fighting.png)

## How it works

- Fill the wagon with crude oil, heavy oil or light oil. It holds 25,000 units.
- Each flamethrower keeps its tank full from the wagon by itself.
- The flamethrowers cover all sides and turn and unfold faster than a flamethrower turret.
- They also reach enemies right next to the train (minimum range 2 instead of 6). The wagon itself is fireproof, other wagons near the flames are not.
- Flamethrower damage research applies as usual.
- The wagon's quality carries over to its flamethrowers.
- Works on elevated rails.

## Unlocking

Research **Flamethrower wagon** after Fluid wagon and Flamethrower. The recipe takes a fluid wagon, two flamethrower turrets and steel.

## Performance

Standing wagons cost nothing per tick. Only moving wagons move their flamethrowers along, and refilling runs twice a second.

## Under the hood

Factorio has no turrets on rolling stock. Every wagon carries two hidden turrets that can't be selected or attacked and that follow the wagon.
